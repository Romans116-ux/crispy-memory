Salt and Grace

A Christian apologetics guide

“Let your conversation be always full of grace, seasoned with salt, so that you may know how to answer everyone.” Colossians 4:6 (NIV)

Salt and Grace helps Christians understand and share the reasons for their faith, and helps seekers explore the evidence for Christianity. Each of its 41 topics gives a short answer, key points, responses to common objections, Scripture, and links to trusted resources for further study.

Frequently asked questions

Which Bible translation does the app use?
Verses are quoted from the NIV by default. You can switch to the KJV or WEB in the app, or choose ESV, NKJV, NASB, CSB, NLT, NET or AMP to read each passage on Bible Gateway.

Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up, and the app collects no personal data.

Where are my saved topics stored?
Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Is the app affiliated with the websites it links to?
No. Salt and Grace links to Got Questions, CARM, Precept Austin, Enduring Word, Wesley Huff, Bible Gateway and Logos for further study, but is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.
