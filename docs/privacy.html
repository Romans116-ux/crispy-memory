Privacy Policy

Salt and Grace · Last updated October 25, 2026

Salt and Grace does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information.

What the app stores

  • No accounts. The app does not require sign-up or login.
  • No analytics or tracking. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software.
  • On-device storage only. Topics you bookmark and your preferred Bible translation are stored locally on your device and are deleted when you delete the app. They are never sent to us or anyone else.

External links

When you tap a link to Bible Gateway, Got Questions, CARM, Precept Austin, Enduring Word, Wesley Huff, Logos or a web search, the page opens in an in-app browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies, which govern any information they collect.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
