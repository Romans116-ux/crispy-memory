# Privacy Policy — Salt and Grace

_Last updated: September 30, 2026_

Salt and Grace does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information.

- **No accounts.** The app does not require sign-up or login.
- **No analytics or tracking.** The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
- **On-device storage only.** Topics you bookmark are stored locally on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.
- **External links.** When you tap a link to Bible Gateway, Got Questions, CARM, Precept Austin, Enduring Word, Wesley Huff, Logos or a web search, that page opens in an in-app browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which govern any data they collect.

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the support URL listed on the App Store page.
